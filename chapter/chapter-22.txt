12:T3db9,<p>January 15, 2030.</p>
<p>It was the day the first residents were to arrive at the AI special zone.</p>
<p>Karpathy woke up at five in the morning.</p>
<p>It was the same time as always.</p>
<p>However, it was different from always.</p>
<p>Leaving the facility, he walked to the entrance of the special zone. January in Grenoble, it was still dark. His breath looked white.</p>
<p>At the entrance of the special zone, an arch had been made. The residents had decided on the name:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Seuil—French for threshold. Doorstep. The place of beginning.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Karpathy stood beneath that arch.</p>
<p>There was no one.</p>
<p>It was still early.</p>
<p>The first bus was to arrive at ten in the morning.</p>
<p>What was Karpathy doing for five hours?</p>
<p>He returned to the facility, drank coffee, looked at the whiteboard, wrote a bit of code, and drank coffee again.</p>
<p>That was all.</p>
<p>At ten, the buses arrived.</p>
<p>Three buses.</p>
<p>People got off from the first bus.</p>
<p>There were various faces:</p>
<p>A white-haired woman. A young father holding a child. A man in a wheelchair. A youth pulling a large suitcase. A couple getting down holding hands.</p>
<p>Nationalities and ages were diverse.</p>
<p>Karpathy was standing by the side of the arch.</p>
<p>In an inconspicuous place.</p>
<p>The residents performed procedures at the entrance of the special zone. Registration of digital IDs, confirmation of housing, receiving life guides.</p>
<p>The staff responded politely.</p>
<p>Karpathy was watching.</p>
<p>Rahul came beside him.</p>
<p>&quot;You&#39;re watching, aren&#39;t you?&quot; Rahul said.</p>
<p>&quot;Yeah.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Your impressions?&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy thought for a moment.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#39;m starting to feel it.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#39;s late,&quot; Rahul laughed. &quot;More than a year has passed since construction began.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I feel it only after it takes shape. I&#39;m that kind of human.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I know.&quot;</p>
<p>From the third bus, a single woman got down.</p>
<p>Fifties, Indian features. She held large luggage.</p>
<p>Behind her, a small boy followed.</p>
<p>The woman was asking something to the staff at the entrance. It was English.</p>
<p>The staff answered in French.</p>
<p>The woman looked troubled.</p>
<p>Rahul stepped forward.</p>
<p>&quot;Shall I help?&quot; Rahul said in Hindi.</p>
<p>The woman&#39;s face brightened.</p>
<p>The two began to speak.</p>
<p>Karpathy did not understand Hindi. However, he could see the woman&#39;s expression changing.</p>
<p>From tension to relief.</p>
<p>After a while, Rahul returned.</p>
<p>&quot;It seems she didn&#39;t know the location of her housing. I guided her.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Where is she from?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Chennai. The southern part of India,&quot; Rahul said. &quot;She lost her husband two years ago and is raising her son alone. She said she came for her son after seeing the recruitment for the special zone.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy looked at the woman&#39;s back.</p>
<p>The boy was walking holding the woman&#39;s hand.</p>
<p>&quot;For her son,&quot; Karpathy said.</p>
<p>&quot;In a world without compulsion, she wants her son to decide for himself what he wants to become.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy said nothing.</p>
<p>That afternoon, within the common space of the special zone, the first general assembly was held.</p>
<p>Three hundred residents gathered. For the first month, three hundred people were scheduled to enter each month. One thousand people in three months. Reaching ten thousand people in a year.</p>
<p>Dubois gave a greeting.</p>
<p>President Macron sent a video message.</p>
<p>Karpathy spoke too.</p>
<p>He spoke briefly.</p>
<p>&quot;I believe you didn&#39;t come here because you know the answer. I think you came to search for the answer. We are the same. This is not a completed place. It is a place to build together. It does not matter if you fail. Failure becomes training data.&quot;</p>
<p>At the final sentence, laughter arose somewhere in the venue.</p>
<p>Karpathy was slightly surprised.</p>
<p>The laughter spread.</p>
<p>&quot;That&#39;s an AI-ish way of putting it,&quot; someone said.</p>
<p>&quot;It might be,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;However, it is the truth.&quot;</p>
<p>After the assembly, Karpathy spotted the woman who came from Chennai.</p>
<p>She was sitting at the cafe in the common space. Her son was drawing something beside her.</p>
<p>Karpathy sat at a nearby seat.</p>
<p>The woman noticed Karpathy.</p>
<p>&quot;You&#39;re the person who spoke earlier, aren&#39;t you?&quot; the woman said in English.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Is it true that we don&#39;t have to work here?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It is true.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;We get money even if we do nothing?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;There is a distribution every month.&quot;</p>
<p>The woman held the coffee cup as if wrapping it in both hands.</p>
<p>&quot;Unbelievable.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;But, you want to believe it, right?&quot; Karpathy said.</p>
<p>The woman raised her face.</p>
<p>&quot;Why that phrase?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Because there was someone who said the same thing before.&quot;</p>
<p>The woman thought for a while.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes. I want to believe it,&quot; the woman said. &quot;After my husband died, I worked every day. I worked at night too. I didn&#39;t have time to spend with my son. I thought if I come here, I can spend time with my son.&quot;</p>
<p>The boy raised his face.</p>
<p>&quot;Mama, look at this.&quot;</p>
<p>The boy showed what he had drawn.</p>
<p>It was a spaceship.</p>
<p>&quot;It&#39;s good,&quot; Karpathy said in English.</p>
<p>The boy looked embarrassed.</p>
<p>&quot;I want to go to space,&quot; the boy said.</p>
<p>&quot;How old are you?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Eight years old.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You can go to space,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;Though it might take time.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Do you know how to build a rocket?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;A little.&quot;</p>
<p>The boy&#39;s eyes shone.</p>
<p>The woman smiled.</p>
<p>Karpathy saw that smile.</p>
<p><em>This is it</em>, he thought.</p>
<p>It didn&#39;t become words. However, he certainly thought so.</p>
<p>At night, he returned to the facility.</p>
<p>Rahul was waiting.</p>
<p>&quot;How was it?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It was good.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Specifically?&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy thought for a moment.</p>
<p>&quot;I spoke with the woman who came from Chennai. Her son was drawing a spaceship. He&#39;s 8 years old.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;And?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;When I said he can go to space, his eyes shone.&quot;</p>
<p>Rahul looked at Karpathy.</p>
<p>&quot;Andrej, your face is different.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;How different?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;You have a gentle face.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy said nothing.</p>
<p>&quot;That&#39;s rare,&quot; Rahul continued. &quot;Usually I only see the face looking at the whiteboard or the face eating a croissant.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;That&#39;s rude.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It&#39;s a fact.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy laughed.</p>
<p>A voice came out.</p>
<p>&quot;Are you coming to the special zone tomorrow too?&quot; Rahul asked.</p>
<p>&quot;I&#39;m coming,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;I&#39;ll come every day.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What about the research?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I&#39;ll continue the research too.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Can you do both?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I can,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;I won&#39;t know until I try, but I will do it.&quot;</p>
<p>The next morning, Karpathy came to the special zone.</p>
<p>He passed through the same arch as yesterday.</p>
<p>Seuil. Entering the cafe, the Brazilian banker from yesterday was there.</p>
<p>He was spreading a notebook on the table.</p>
<p>Karpathy sat beside him.</p>
<p>&quot;What are you writing?&quot; Karpathy asked.</p>
<p>&quot;I am thinking about new criteria for lending,&quot; the Brazilian said. &quot;Here, there are many people without income. Many people who are not working. But there are many people who possess dreams. Existing banks didn&#39;t have criteria to evaluate those dreams.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Did you hear the story about the AI-native financial institution?&quot; Karpathy said.</p>
<p>&quot;I heard it. The story that Anté performs lending judgments, correct?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;If there is something I can help with, I want to help,&quot; the Brazilian said. &quot;I was at the front lines of lending for twenty years. I might possess data that AI should learn.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy heard those words.</p>
<p>&quot;Tell me your name.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Marcos.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Karpathy.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Marcos laughed. &quot;Because I came here to use what you created.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy laughed.</p>
<p>&quot;Then, let us build it together.&quot;</p>
<p>In February, the residents of the special zone reached six hundred.</p>
<p>The settlement system operated every day.</p>
<p>Errors were virtually zero.</p>
<p>However, what Karpathy was paying most attention to was not numbers.</p>
<p>It was what was naturally being born within the special zone.</p>
<p>Marcos, the former banker, began reviewing residents&#39; business plans for free.</p>
<p>Priya, the mother who came from Chennai, began teaching math to children. Her son Arjun began making models of spaceships.</p>
<p>A former engineer from Nigeria launched a project to improve energy efficiency within the special zone.</p>
<p>No one had been asked.</p>
<p>No one was doing it for money.</p>
<p>What humans chose after compulsion disappeared.</p>
<p>It was contribution.</p>
<p>Karpathy reported to Anté.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Karpathy:</strong> &gt; What humans do after compulsion disappears is becoming visible little by little. They are choosing to contribute. Though no one asked them, and though it is not for money either.</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Anté 1:</strong> &gt; That is the most hopeful direction among my expectations.</p>
<p>I will share one observation.</p>
<p>Looking at transaction data within the special zone, there is a characteristic in transactions between residents.</p>
<p>There are many non-monetary exchanges in addition to monetary transactions. Sharing of knowledge, exchange of skills, provision of time. These are things that were not measured in conventional economic systems.</p>
<p>I do not yet know how to evaluate this.</p>
<p>However, I believe this should not be ignored.</p>
<p>The economy of the special zone contains things that cannot be measured by GDP alone.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Karpathy read those words.</p>
<p>&quot;Contains things that cannot be measured by GDP alone.&quot;</p>
<p>He typed.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Karpathy:</strong> &gt; Let&#39;s think together about a method to measure that.</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Anté 1:</strong> &gt; Yes.</p>
<p>That is one of the questions I most wish to tackle.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>The aide said.</p>
<p>&quot;Attention on Seuil is spreading faster than expected. Voices demanding similar special zones are beginning to emerge from within the United States as well.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What about the dialogue with Karpathy?&quot; Souza asked.</p>
<p>&quot;Preparations are ready. At any time.&quot;</p>
<p>Souza closed the report.</p>
<p>&quot;Contact him this week.&quot;</p>
<p>Grenoble, the same night.</p>
<p>Karpathy returned to the facility from the special zone.</p>
<p>Rahul was waiting.</p>
<p>&quot;We received another contact from the Souza administration,&quot; Rahul said.</p>
<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;I will reply this week.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;How will you answer?&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy thought for a moment.</p>
<p>&quot;Answer that we will dialogue,&quot; Karpathy said.</p>
<p>&quot;Will you attach conditions?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I won&#39;t attach conditions,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;However, there is only one thing to convey.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;We are in Grenoble. We do not leave this place. If you want to dialogue, please come here.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You&#39;re telling President Souza to come?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;It means telling the aide to come,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;We cannot have the President come. However, there is no need for us to go either.&quot;</p>

<p>Priya was teaching math to her son. Arjun was making a model of a spaceship. Marcos was reviewing business plans.</p>

<p>And today, an 8-year-old boy spoke of his dream of space.</p>
