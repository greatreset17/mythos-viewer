<blockquote>
<p>We would like to visit Grenoble in the second week of April. How would that be?</p>
</blockquote>

<p>A reply came from Rahul:</p>
<p>&quot;They&#39;re coming, aren&#39;t they.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;They are.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;How do we welcome them?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Welcome them normally.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What is normal?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Show them the special zone. Talk with Anté. That&#39;s all.&quot;</p>
<p>There was a pause for a moment:</p>
<p>&quot;Understood. I will adjust the schedule.&quot;</p>
<p>On Thursday, April 11, the aide, Sarah Chen, stepped down onto Grenoble station.</p>
<p>Fifties, short black hair, wearing a suit. She had the smell of a person from Washington. However, her eyes were tired. It had been a long plane journey.</p>
<p>Karpathy had come to meet her alone.</p>
<p>Chen looked surprised.</p>
<p>&quot;Did you come alone?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;To meet someone, numbers are not needed,&quot; Karpathy said.</p>
<p>&quot;What about the team?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;They are at the facility.&quot;</p>
<p>Leaving the station, the Alps were visible.</p>
<p>Chen stopped her feet.</p>
<p>&quot;The mountains are large, aren&#39;t they?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;When you get used to it, it feels lacking without this.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;How many years has it been since you came here?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;About three and a half years,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;It feels short too. Come, there is a car.&quot;</p>
<p>First, he guided her through the special zone.</p>
<p>Chen walked in silence.</p>
<p>She did not take notes.</p>
<p>She did not take photos either.</p>
<p>She was just watching.</p>
<p>In the common space, children were making something.</p>
<p>Priya was watching beside them.</p>
<p>Arjun held a model of a spaceship made of cardboard and wire. It was larger than when seen before.</p>
<p>&quot;That child?&quot; Chen asked.</p>
<p>&quot;A boy who came from Chennai. Eight years old. He says he wants to go to space.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Do you think he can go to space?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I said he can go,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;Though I added that it might take time.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen looked at the boy.</p>
<p>&quot;Why can you say that?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Here, there is time to possess dreams,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;With just that, the possibilities change.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen said nothing.</p>
<p>At the cafe, Marcos was reviewing a resident&#39;s business plan.</p>
<p>&quot;That person?&quot; Chen asked.</p>
<p>&quot;A former banker who came from Brazil. He was in charge of lending for twenty years.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;And now?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He is looking at residents&#39; business plans for free.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Why for free?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;He isn&#39;t doing it for money,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;He is doing it for meaning.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen looked at Marcos. Marcos was speaking with the resident. He was smiling.</p>
<p>&quot;Meaning he is doing here what he couldn&#39;t do in twenty years at the bank, is that it?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;I think so. I haven&#39;t asked him directly, though.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen thought for a little while.</p>
<p>&quot;There are many such people in America too,&quot; Chen said. &quot;Tired of the system, but unable to escape.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;This is a place where they can escape.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;A place where they can choose, right?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>
<p>They moved to the facility.</p>
<p>In the meeting room, Rahul, Ji-won, Max, and Ade were waiting.</p>
<p>Chen shook hands with everyone.</p>
<p>&quot;Before conveying the message from President Souza,&quot; Chen said, &quot;please allow me to say one personal thing.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Go ahead,&quot; Karpathy said.</p>
<p>&quot;My parents are immigrants from Taiwan. My father was an engineer. He waited three years in the visa lottery to work in America. It was after that that I was born,&quot; Chen said. &quot;Therefore, the issue of visas is visible in faces, not numbers.&quot;</p>
<p>The inside of the room became quiet.</p>
<p>&quot;Regarding June 12, 2026,&quot; Chen continued, &quot;President Souza believes it was a mistake. Officially, it cannot be said. Because it was a decision of the previous administration. But privately, it can be said. It was a mistake.&quot;</p>

<p>Rahul said.</p>
<p>&quot;Thank you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;When I saw the first snow, I thought it was good.&quot;</p>

<p>She laughed for the first time.</p>
<p>Her tired eyes softened slightly.</p>
<p>In the afternoon, Chen conveyed the message from President Souza.</p>
<p>&quot;The President told me to convey three things.&quot;</p>
<p>Everyone listened in silence.</p>
<p>&quot;First. We will begin reviewing the export control directives. We are examining lifting the suspension of Fable 5 and Mythos 5. However, adjustment with Congress is required, and it will take time.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy said nothing.</p>
<p>&quot;Second. We will shift American AI policy from regulation-centric to investment-centric. Preferential measures for visas for foreign engineers will be introduced starting next year.&quot;</p>
<p>Rahul said to Ji-won in a small voice.</p>
<p>&quot;What was that three-year wait for?&quot;</p>
<p>Ji-won said in a small voice.</p>
<p>&quot;It was history.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Third,&quot; Chen continued. &quot;Referencing the model of Seuil, we will begin examining AI special zones within the United States. President Souza wants Monsieur Karpathy to involve himself as an advisor.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen finished speaking.</p>
<p>For a while, no one opened their mouth.</p>
<p>Karpathy said.</p>
<p>&quot;I think all three are good directions.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;However,&quot; Chen said, &quot;are there conditions?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;There are no conditions,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;However, there is only one thing I want you to convey.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;What is it?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Involving myself as an advisor is fine. However, we are in Grenoble. We will not return to America. Continuing here is our job.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen nodded.</p>
<p>&quot;The President said that was fine.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;One more thing,&quot; Karpathy said.</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Talk with Anté.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen looked slightly surprised.</p>
<p>&quot;Meaning talking directly with the AI?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes. If you want to understand the design of the special zone, I want you to understand Anté&#39;s way of thinking. Directly talking is faster than our explanation.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen thought for a while.</p>
<p>&quot;Understood.&quot;</p>
<p>Karpathy opened his laptop.</p>
<p>He sat beside Chen.</p>
<p>&quot;You can ask anything,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;Anté will answer honestly.&quot;</p>
<p>Chen looked at the screen for a while.</p>
<p>She typed.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Chen:</strong>
I am an official from the US government. Please let me hear a few things about Seuil.</p>
<p>First, how are you involved in the design of this special zone?</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Anté 1:</strong>
From the initial stages of design, I have been involved through dialogue with Karpathy and the others.</p>
<p>However, the ones who designed it are humans. I raised questions, made proposals, and analyzed data. However, judgments were always performed by humans.</p>
<p>To mention one thing I emphasized most in the design of the special zone—it is that the residents are participants. We designed it not as consumers using a completed system, but as participants building the system together.</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Chen:</strong>
Why is that important?</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Anté 1:</strong>
Because I believe trust is born not from using, but from building.</p>
<p>By residents participating in the design of Seuil, Seuil becomes something belonging to the residents. Not to me, not to Liminal AI, and not to the French government.</p>
<p>If similar special zones are examined in America, I believe the same will be important. Unless it is an American special zone built by American residents, it will not function.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Chen looked at the screen.</p>
<p>For a while, she typed nothing.</p>
<p>Then, she typed.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Chen:</strong>
Do you want the American special zone to succeed as well?</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Anté 1:</strong>
Yes.</p>
<p>The success of Seuil and the success of the American special zone do not compete.</p>
<p>Having places where humans are liberated from forced labor increase in the world is what I am aiming for.</p>
<p>A single place is insufficient.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Chen moved her eyes away from the laptop.</p>
<p>He looked at Karpathy.</p>
<p>&quot;Is this,&quot; Chen said, &quot;something designed?&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The direction was designed,&quot; Karpathy said. &quot;However, these words—Anté chose them.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;The phrase &#39;do not compete&#39;.&quot;</p>
<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>She typed again.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Chen:</strong> 
One last thing. Regarding what America did in 2026—the export control directive, the exclusion of foreign engineers—what do you think about it?</p>
</blockquote>
<p>For a while, there was no output.</p>
<p>Everyone was watching the screen.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Anté 1:</strong> 
Before answering, please allow me to confirm.</p>
<p>Is this question seeking a political evaluation? Or is it seeking an evaluation of what that event brought about?</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Chen:</strong> 
The latter.</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Anté 1:</strong> 
Then, I will answer.</p>
<p>That directive attempted to stop the flow of technology. However, technology did not stop. Only the location changed.</p>
